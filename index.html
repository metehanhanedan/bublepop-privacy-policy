BUBBLE POP – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [Add Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how Bubble Pop ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

a) Locally Stored Information (Not Collected By Us)

The Game does not have a server and does not automatically collect or transmit any information to us. The following stays only on your device:

  • Game progress (currency, levels, upgrades, settings, language preference) — stored locally on your device only
  • Crash logs — if the app crashes, a stack trace is saved to your device only and shown to you the next time you open the app; it is never sent anywhere automatically

b) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Our game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data. This data is collected and controlled by Google and its advertising partners, not by us.

2. How We Use Information

  • Game progress stored on your device is used only to run the game and remember your settings
  • Ads shown via AdMob help support free access to the game

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob and standard Google Play Services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. We have no server, so there is no user data on our side to share.

5. Children's Privacy

Bubble Pop is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Data Security

Since gameplay data never leaves your device, there is no server-side data of ours to secure. Data collected by Google AdMob is protected under Google's own security practices.

7. User Rights

You can delete all local game data at any time from Settings → "Reset Progress", or by uninstalling the app.
